# Community expectations

Everyone is welcome to ask questions, learn and contribute. Assume good intent,
explain unfamiliar terms, and criticize ideas and code rather than people.
Respect different experience levels, backgrounds and accessibility needs.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, spam and unwanted contact
are not welcome. Do not post private information or pressure contributors to
provide work or support. Keep discussion constructive and relevant.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, lock discussions or restrict participation
when necessary. Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abuse; do not repost
private details in public reports. Moderation should be fair and proportionate.
